Privacy Policy

Material Calculator · Last updated September 12, 2026

Material Calculator is a utility app for estimating how much paint, tile, and concrete a home improvement job needs. This page explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

The short version

Material Calculator has no accounts and no sign-in. Everything you type — room dimensions, tile sizes, prices — is used only to compute your result on your own device and is never sent anywhere. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which is the only third party involved and the only reason the app connects to the internet at all.

Information we don't collect

The app does not require or ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or any other personal information. There is no account system and no server that the app talks to for its calculators.

Information stored on your device

A small amount of information is saved locally on your phone, using Android's standard on-device storage, so the app remembers your preferences between sessions:

  • Your chosen unit system (metric or imperial)
  • Your chosen currency for cost estimates
  • A short summary of your most recent calculation (e.g. "18 bags of concrete for your slab"), so the home screen can offer to pick up where you left off

None of this ever leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

Advertising

Material Calculator is free to use and supported by ads served through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob and Google may collect technical information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, and ad interaction data. This app does not control what AdMob collects — that's governed by Google's own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner sites and apps

You can review or reset your advertising identifier, and opt out of personalized ads, from your device's system settings (usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your Android version).

Permissions

The app requests only the Internet permission, used exclusively to request and display ads. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

Children's privacy

Material Calculator is a general-audience tool aimed at DIYers and contractors and is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We don't sell your data — there isn't any of yours on our end to sell. The only outside party the app interacts with is Google AdMob, for the sole purpose of serving ads, as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a new feature that changes what data is collected — this page will be updated and the "last updated" date at the top will reflect the change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to vectorwiseco@gmail.com.
